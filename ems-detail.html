Project Description

Developed a mobile service marketplace app for an Arabic client, featuring Google Maps integration for service discovery and provider management. The app enables users to:

  • Find service providers on an interactive Google Map
  • Send offers to providers by selecting them from the map
  • Providers can accept offers, chat, and finalize deals
  • Provider panel for managing offers and completing jobs
  • Real-time chat and notifications
The project demonstrates expertise in mobile development, map integration, real-time communication, and marketplace workflows tailored for a multilingual audience.
